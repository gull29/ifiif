Slope Unblocked

4.2/5
1,452 VOTES

Slope is one long fall. You steer a ball down a neon course that keeps tilting away beneath you, and you have exactly two inputs: left and right. There is no jump, no brake and no checkpoint. A run lasts until you clip a red block or miss the track entirely, and then it begins again at zero.

The difficulty is acceleration. Speed climbs continuously for as long as you stay alive, so the course is not really getting harder — your reaction window is getting shorter. The same gap you drifted through comfortably at the start becomes something you have to commit to a full second early, and steering inputs that felt gentle begin to throw you off the edge.

Your score is distance, which makes every run directly comparable to the last one. The build guards that number: it saves through Unity's ObscuredPrefs, an anti-cheat wrapper that stops the stored high score being edited locally. Slope was published by Y8.

How to play Slope?

Steer in short taps rather than holding a direction, because at speed a held input turns into a much bigger movement than you intended. Favour the middle of the track when you are not actively avoiding something, so you have room to correct in either direction. Look at the far end of the course rather than at the ball — by the time an obstacle is near the ball, the decision about it has already been made.

Controls

Desktop: Use the left and right arrow keys or A and D to steer.
